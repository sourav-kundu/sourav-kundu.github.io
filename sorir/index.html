Why Sorir

You already run a health OS.
It's just held together by hand.

A serious health routine today is a stack of apps that don't remember you and don't talk to each other. Sorir replaces the stack with one system.

What you can do

Understand your body, then do something about it

Sorir turns everything you track into answers about you, and a clear next step.

Ask anything about your health

Get answers from your own data, not generic advice, with sources you can tap and check.

Never repeat yourself

Tell Sorir you started a supplement or hurt your knee. It remembers, and it keeps up when things change.

Hear about what matters

When a report moves, a goal slips or a pattern appears, Sorir tells you first, without nagging.

Know your real age

Sorir Age shows how fit you are for your age. Blood Age shows what your blood work says. Both show what would move them.

Reach any goal

Set a goal on weight, steps, sleep, protein or a lab value, and see every day whether you are on pace.

Make sense of your lab reports

Upload PDFs or photos. Every value becomes a trend you can follow, shown against a healthy range.

Food

Log food the way you'd say it

Type it, say it or take a photo. Sorir understands how people really eat, including Indian dishes and everyday household measures.

  • Describe a whole meal in plain language and log it in seconds.
  • Snap a plate for an estimate, or a nutrition label to save a food exactly as printed.
  • Your calories and macros stay in step with Apple Health.
Energy

See where your day stands

Calories in, calories out, macros and workouts in one view, with trends across weeks and months.

  • Your calorie balance at a glance, at any point in the day.
  • Weekly and monthly trends, with your weight alongside.
  • Pick which device counts for each metric, so your numbers always agree.
Recovery

Know when to push and when to rest

Liveliness is your daily readiness score, measured against your own normal rather than a population average.

  • A simple score for how ready you are today.
  • See what is lifting your score and what is holding it back.
  • A plain one-line read on the day ahead.
Sleep

See how you really slept

A nightly sleep score that shows what went well, what didn't, and how your sleep is trending.

  • One clear score for every night.
  • Follow your sleep stages across the night.
  • Set sleep goals and watch them over time.
