# sourav-kundu.github.io — public website (contributor & AI-agent guide)

This repository is **PUBLIC**. It is served on the open web at https://sourav-kundu.com and
https://sourav-kundu.github.io. **Everything committed here is world-readable, permanently** — and may
be cached or indexed by third parties even after it is deleted. Treat every commit as irreversible publication.

## HARD RULE — public-safe, professional content only (never break this)

Only polished, **professional, public-safe** content belongs in this repo. **Never** add anything
private or personal. When in doubt, **leave it out.**

**Never publish here — for the site owner or anyone else:**
- **Compensation & finances** — salary, base/bonus/variable, equity / RSUs / ESOPs, offers, comp
  comparisons, retention bonuses, valuations, or any money detail.
- **Private career context** — job-search or hiring activity, applications, target companies,
  recruiter or interview notes, or career/trajectory planning.
- **Opinions or sentiment about any company or employer** — current, past, or prospective; positive
  *or* negative (likes, dislikes, criticism, "reasons to move," growth/leadership complaints). All of
  this stays private.
- **Internal or confidential company information** — internal levels, org internals, confidential metrics.
- **Personal / private detail** — family, health, finances, home address, phone number.

This site is a **professional showcase only**. The source of truth for any private material lives in
**separate private working files outside this repo** and must **never** be copied in.

**Before any commit, scan the changed files against the list above.** If anything is borderline, do not
commit it — confirm with the owner first.
